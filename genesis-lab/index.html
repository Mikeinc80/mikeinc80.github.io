Genesis Lab03

Proof belongs in the open.

Genesis Lab is the publication and evidence layer for Premium AI Genesis products, pilots, prototypes, and research. It is designed to separate what is operational from what is exploratory.

Status Registry01

Five classifications, each with a fixed public meaning.

A label is applied only when its evidence requirement is already satisfied. Nothing is promoted to a stronger status to appear further along.

LIVLive
Publicly available or verifiably operating in its stated context.
PLTPilot
A bounded implementation with defined users, scope, and evaluation criteria.
PRTPrototype
A functional concept build that is not represented as production-ready.
RSCResearch
An investigation or technical exploration; not a product or deployment.
CSNComing Soon
An authorized release with a defined public scope.

Current Registry02

No public releases are listed.

The absence is intentional. Genesis Lab will publish work only when its status, scope, ownership, and evidence can be represented accurately. An empty registry is a more useful signal than an invented one.

Publication Standard03

What every future entry must state.

The standard exists now so the first published entry is held to it, rather than written around it.

  1. 01

    What exists

    The system, product, or investigation, described in concrete terms.

  2. 02

    Who it is for

    The intended user or operating context.

  3. 03

    Status

    One classification from the registry, with nothing implied beyond it.

  4. 04

    Evidence

    What substantiates the status — access, users, measurements, or published output.

  5. 05

    Limitations

    What the entry does not do, and what remains unproven.

  6. 06

    Last reviewed

    The date the record was verified as still accurate.
